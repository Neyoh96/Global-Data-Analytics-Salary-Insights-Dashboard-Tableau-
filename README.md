Overview
In the present report, the most prominent findings are summarized using a dashboard demonstrating salary patterns in the fields of AI, Machine Learning, and Data Science (probably on the basis of a global sample over the last few years, e.g., on the scale of AI job salary datasets distributed by Kaggle). The dashboard gives a breakdown of experience level, job title, company size, employment type, where the employees live and the average salary per country. All salaries are in USD.
The statistics reveal a competitive job market that is full time in medium to large organizations and large differences in salary according to experience, job specialization, and place of residence (mostly higher in North America).

Distribution of Levels of Experience.

Senior: ~46%

The proportion of senior positions comprises almost half of the set of data, which suggests the presence of a mature workforce.

Lessons and Pro suggestions.

Salary Progression: Salaries grow tremendously with experience and specialization (e.g. ML/AI-related jobs are premium-paid).
Location Impact: US/Canada based companies can significantly increase profits by relocating or working remotely.
Market trends: The senior talent is in high demand; entry level positions are competitive but have high growth potential.
Data Source: It seems to be comparable to publicly available datasets on websites such as Kaggle (e.g., Global AI Job Market and Salary Trends).
